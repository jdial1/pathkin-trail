## Full description

The Pathkins are leaving their old burrows to find a home of their own, and
they need someone to lead them.

Along the Trail, every Stop is a different kind of puzzle with a hidden rule.
Which bridge will hold which Pathkin? What does the keeper want to eat? In
what order must the line stand? You are never told. You try something, watch
what happens, and work it out. A wrong try is a clue, not a dead end.

- Fifteen kinds of logic puzzle, seventeen puzzles, four levels each
- A roster of 36 or 100 Pathkins, each a different mix of five traits
- Three legs through meadow, canyon and peak, with a fork in the middle
- Camps where Pathkins rest, and where you can bring in any who got stranded
- A Daily Stop, a practice mode, goals, trinkets and a Collection Book
- A picture maker with stickers
- Plays fully offline. No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no tracking
- English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese
- Built to be readable: large text option, colour-safe coats, less-motion
  option, and no timers anywhere

For ages 7 and up.

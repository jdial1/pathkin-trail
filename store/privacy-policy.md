# Privacy policy (draft)

This is a draft for legal review. It describes what the game does today; it
is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

**Pathkin Trail collects no personal information.**

- The game does not ask for a name, an email address, an account or a birthday.
- The game cannot use the internet. The Android app does not request network
  permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- There are no advertisements, no advertising identifiers, no analytics
  services and no third-party trackers.
- There are no purchases inside the game.

## What is stored on the device

- Saved games, settings, the Collection Book and the play record (which
  puzzles were played and how they went). These stay on the device. The play
  record can be erased from Settings, and uninstalling the game removes all of
  it.
- Nicknames a player gives to creatures are stored only in the saved game.
- A picture made in the picture maker is saved to the device's Pictures folder
  only when the player taps "Save picture". The game does not read any other
  pictures.

## Permissions

- Vibration, for touch feedback. It can be turned off in Settings.

## Children

The game is suitable for children. Because it collects nothing and cannot
connect to the internet, there is no data about children to protect, share or
delete. A legal review should confirm how this maps onto the children's-privacy
rules of each region where the game is sold.

## Changes

If a later version ever adds an optional way to share play statistics, it will
be off by default, anonymous, limited to puzzle id, level, attempts, hints and
solve time, and this policy will be updated before that version ships.
